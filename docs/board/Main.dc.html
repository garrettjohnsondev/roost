Roost

A group chat
with your crew

The conference already produces a plan, a critique and a reconciliation. Three turns, three agents, each answering the last — that is a conversation. The build renders it as a formal ceremony in identical cards. Render it as a thread and the product explains itself: you watch Pip size the job, Ollie draft, Juno push back, Ollie concede.

How the thread behaves

  • Three poses per agent — idle, typing, thinking — swapped on a hard cut. Pixel art never cross-fades.
  • Idle breathes at 1.5 s; working drops to 420 ms. Speed is the status.
  • A typing bubble belongs to whoever is actually mid-turn, so the wait is legible instead of a spinner.
  • You are a participant: your photo, your name, your side of the thread.
  • Reduced motion freezes every sprite to frame one.

Second pass — what I threw out

Near-black and one acid accent. The second-commonest generated look. Indigo comes from the product's name instead, so the ground is a material rather than an absence.
Smooth gradient fuel bars. Replaced with counted blocks: faster to read at arm's length, and they can't imply precision the quota API doesn't have.
Keeping the cards. Sections are divided by a seam now. A card around everything is how four unrelated things came to look identical.
Round iMessage bubbles. Borrowed the grammar, not the styling: square bubbles with a stepped pixel tail, so the thread belongs to the same world as the sprites.
